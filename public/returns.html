Return & Refund Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

We want you to be happy with your VODAR product. If something is not right, this policy explains how returns, exchanges and refunds work.

1. Right of withdrawal (EU/UK customers)

If you are a consumer in the EU or UK, you have the right to withdraw from your purchase within 14 days of receiving your order, without giving a reason. To exercise this right, submit a request with your order number through the Track Order section on our website, or use our contact form, within the 14-day period.

2. How to start a return or exchange

  1. Open the Track Order section and enter your order number.
  2. Fill in the support form (choose Return, Exchange or Other issue) using the email address you ordered with.
  3. We will reply with the return instructions and the return address.

Please do not send anything back before we confirm your return — unauthorized packages cannot be accepted.

3. Condition of returned items

  • Items must be unused and in their original packaging with all accessories.
  • For hygiene reasons, worn bands/straps and unsealed personal-wear items cannot be returned unless they are defective.
  • Gift cards and items marked as final sale cannot be returned.

4. Return shipping costs

Unless the item is defective or we made a mistake, you bear the direct cost of returning the goods. If the item is defective or was shipped in error, we cover the return shipping.

5. Refunds

Once we receive and inspect your return, we refund the full amount to your original payment method. Refunds are issued manually and typically arrive within 14 days of us receiving the returned item. We may deduct a proportional amount if the value of the item has diminished due to handling beyond what is necessary to establish its nature, characteristics and function.

6. Defective items & warranty

If your product arrives damaged or develops a fault, contact us with your order number and a short description (photos help). We offer a free replacement or repair for manufacturing defects. Shipping for defective items is on us.

7. Cancellations

You can cancel an order free of charge any time before it ships. Contact us via the contact form with your order number.

8. Contact

For any return, exchange or refund question, use the contact form on our website and include your order number.
