Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how VODAR (“we”, “us”) collects, uses and protects your personal data when you use our website vodar.net and shop with us. We keep this policy short and plain on purpose.

1. Who we are

VODAR operates this online store. If you have any privacy question or request, contact us through the contact form on our website — please include your order number (if applicable) so we can help you faster.

2. What data we collect

  • Order data — when you place an order we collect your name, email address, shipping address (street, city, country, postal code) and the items you purchase.
  • Support data — when you submit an after-sales or support request we collect the request content and the email address used for the order.
  • Message data — when you use the contact form we collect your email address and your message.

We do not run advertising or analytics trackers on this website.

3. Why we use your data

  • To process and fulfil your order, including international shipping.
  • To send you the order confirmation email and order updates.
  • To handle returns, exchanges and support requests.
  • To keep transaction records where we are legally required to do so.

We never sell your personal data.

4. Payment data

Card payments are processed by our payment provider. We never see or store your full card number. We only receive the payment status and the amount paid.

5. Cookies

This website uses no tracking cookies. The only cookie we set is a strictly necessary session cookie used by the store operator to sign in to the admin panel. It is not used for buyers and is required for the site to function.

6. Where your data is stored and who processes it

  • Website, orders and database — Cloudflare (Pages / D1).
  • Payment processing — our payment provider (LianLian Global).
  • Transactional email — Resend.
  • Contact form — Web3Forms.

Some of these providers process data outside your country, including in the United States. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. How long we keep data

Order data is kept for as long as necessary to fulfil the order, handle warranty and return claims, and meet legal accounting obligations (up to 5 years). Contact-form messages are deleted once processed, unless tied to an ongoing order or support case.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the EU/UK GDPR), you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • have it corrected or deleted;
  • restrict or object to processing;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent;
  • lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us via the contact form. We respond within 30 days.

9. Children

This store is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

10. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “last updated” date at the top always reflects the current version.
